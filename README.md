# ❄️ FROSTBYTE – Capture The Flag Hackathon

The holiday season doesn’t end with Christmas 🎄 — we’re carrying the festive spirit straight into the new year with **FROSTBYTE** ❄️, a high-energy Capture The Flag (CTF) Hackathon organized by **ACM RSET Student Chapter** in collaboration with **Offenso Academy**.

FROSTBYTE challenges participants with real-world cybersecurity problems across cryptography, web exploitation, forensics, and more — all packed into an intense, hands-on competitive experience with a **prize pool of ₹20,000**.

---

## 📍 Event Details

- **Venue:** Rajagiri School of Engineering & Technology (RSET)  
- **Date:** 9th – 10th January  
- **Format:** On-site, team-based CTF Hackathon

---

## 👥 Participation

- **Team Size:** 3 members per team  
- Open to students of all skill levels — beginners to experienced CTF players.
